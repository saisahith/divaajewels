## Product Tagging

Each product should have:

- One product type tag, such as `necklace-sets`, `rings`, `sarees`, or `dresses-gowns`.
- One style/type tag, such as `kundan-necklace-sets`, `american-diamond-rings`, or `party-wear-sarees`.
- Optional merchandising tags: `new-arrivals`, `sale`, `clearance`.

Product detail pages are generated automatically by Shopify for every product. The separate pages for Necklace Sets, Rings, Sarees, and the other groups are collection pages, so they must be created as collections in Admin.
